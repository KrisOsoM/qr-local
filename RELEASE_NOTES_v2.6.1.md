# QR Local v2.6.1 — Release Notes

QR Local v2.6.1 is a single-file, offline-capable QR generator focused on privacy, portability and long-term usability without subscriptions.

## Highlights

- Static QR generation for common content types
- PNG and SVG output
- Optional custom colors and logo
- CSV/XLSX batch generation
- ZIP export with manifest
- Local-only statistics by QR type and date
- Activity graphs with no remote analytics
- Responsive statistics layout fix in v2.6.1

## Distribution

The release is distributed as a single `index.html` file. Users may keep it locally and open it in a browser without installing software.

## Privacy

No QR payloads are intentionally transmitted to a remote service. Usage statistics are local to the browser and do not contain QR content.
