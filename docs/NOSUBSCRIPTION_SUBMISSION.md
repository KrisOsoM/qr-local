## Tagline

Private QR generation that works offline.

## Short description

QR Local is a free, open-source, single-file QR generator that runs entirely in the browser. It works offline after download, requires no account, sends no QR content or usage data to the author, exports PNG/SVG, supports URL, text, Wi-Fi, WhatsApp, phone, email and vCard, and can generate batches from CSV/XLSX. Local-only statistics provide useful history without telemetry.

## Key points for review

- 100% free to use.
- Open source under the MIT License.
- No subscriptions.
- No account required.
- No intentional telemetry.
- QR payloads are processed locally.
- CSV/XLSX files are processed locally.
- Single-file offline-capable distribution.
- PNG and SVG export.
- Batch generation with ZIP + manifest.
- Local-only statistics and charts.
- Public source repository.
- Public live demo via GitHub Pages.

## Comparable subscription-based products

Examples include QR TIGER and Uniqode. QR Local is not intended to replicate their hosted dynamic-QR, analytics, campaign-management, or enterprise features; it focuses on private static QR generation with no recurring service dependency.
